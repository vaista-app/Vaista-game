About Us

Vaista Dare Koro To? is a simple friend-group game made for laughs, quick spins and memorable moments together.

Friends. Truth. Dare. Chaos.

Hi, I am Sohan. The developer of this app. Well I got a bunch of ideas before. Last project of mine was creating a message app but honestly i didn’t have any server to connect the users. So that was flop. So then I thought to make my school webpage but nah who cares? After some day, I played Truth and dare with my friends. But we didn’t got any question to ask because we were so close and we knew each others so well . So I thought to make an question app first but wait a minute why not to create an offline truth or dare app? For new odd questions. So I created it. I hope you are going to like it. And best of luck for your life.
